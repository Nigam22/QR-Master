# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-12-21

This Privacy Policy describes how **QR Code Scanner – Barcode Reader** (“the App”) handles information when you use it.

## Summary
- The App can generate QR codes from information you enter and can scan QR/barcodes using your camera (if you allow permission).
- The App may show ads (AdMob). Ad networks may collect device identifiers for advertising and analytics.
- The App can store your scan/generate history locally on your device.
- The App does not require you to create an account.

## Information We Collect

### 1) Information you provide
The App processes the content you enter to generate QR codes (for example: text, URL, phone number, SMS details, WiFi credentials, contact data, UPI details). This data is used only to generate the QR code you request.

### 2) Camera permission (Scan)
If you use the scan feature, the App requests camera access to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are used for scanning in real time.

### 3) Local storage (History)
The App may store scan results and generated QR data **locally on your device** to show your History. You can clear your history from within the App.

### 4) Advertising data (AdMob)
If the App displays ads, third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob) may collect information such as:
- Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
- Approximate location (derived from IP, depending on ad settings)
- App usage events for analytics and ad performance

These partners process data under their own privacy policies.

Google/AdMob: `https://policies.google.com/privacy`

## How We Use Information
- To generate and display QR codes.
- To scan and display results.
- To save and show your scan/generate history on your device.
- To display ads and measure ad performance (if ads are enabled).

## Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal information.

The App may share information with:
- **Advertising providers** (e.g., AdMob) for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and analytics.

## Data Retention
- History data is stored locally until you delete it (via Clear History).
- Advertising/analytics retention is controlled by the third-party provider.

## Security
We take reasonable measures to protect data within the App, but no method of storage is 100% secure.

## Children’s Privacy
The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your Choices
- You can deny camera permission (scan feature may not work).
- You can clear your history from the Settings screen.
- You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (Android/iOS).
